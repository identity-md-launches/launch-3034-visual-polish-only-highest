    How the raid works

    Every number comes from SqueekCore on Sepolia. Nothing here makes Squeektoshi run: the raid only reads his wheel.
    1

    Charge

    While Squeektoshi runs, charge the 10-minute round in progress. One charge per wallet per round, free on Sepolia.

    2

    Settle

    When SqueekCore settles the round, everyone who charged it hits the boss for the round's real wheel work. A still wheel is a miss.

    3

    Topple

    Empty its HP and a stronger boss steps in. Everyone who hurt the fallen boss claims a soulbound trophy. Leave it a week and it flees.

    Rally: +10% per extra raider in a round, up to +50% Squeek holders +25% power Wheeling owners +25% power Trophies: Gold 25%+ of max HP, Silver 8%+, Bronze Killing blow for every raider in the final round
